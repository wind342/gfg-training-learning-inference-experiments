# Third-party literature authorities

The private research archive froze local author-version copies of the two
papers below so that the precise literature authorities used during the GF-P02
preimplementation audits could be identified and hashed. The PDFs are not
redistributed in this public repository because their copyright terms do not
grant this repository permission to republish them.

The existing audit JSON and artifact manifests preserve the historical local
paths and cryptographic identities of the inspected copies. Their absence from
the public tree is intentional and does not affect the executable provenance-
semiring projection, its tests or its recorded results.

1. Buneman, P., Khanna, S. & Tan, W.-C. *Why and Where: A Characterization of
   Data Provenance*. In *Database Theory — ICDT 2001*, 316–330 (Springer,
   2001). DOI: <https://doi.org/10.1007/3-540-44503-X_20>.

   Audited local filename:
   `buneman-khanna-tan-icdt2001-author-version.pdf`

2. Green, T. J., Karvounarakis, G. & Tannen, V. *Provenance Semirings*. In
   *Proceedings of the Twenty-Sixth ACM SIGMOD-SIGACT-SIGART Symposium on
   Principles of Database Systems*, 31–40 (ACM, 2007). DOI:
   <https://doi.org/10.1145/1265530.1265535>.

   Audited local filename:
   `green-karvounarakis-tannen-pods2007-author-version.pdf`

Readers who require the full text should obtain it through the DOI landing
pages, an institutional library or another source authorized by the copyright
holder.
