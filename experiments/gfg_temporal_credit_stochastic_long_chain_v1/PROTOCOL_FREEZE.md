# Stochastic long-chain temporal-credit protocol v1

## Scientific question

RL-E04 changes exactly one scientific variable from RL-E03.  The environment
remains observable, 64-step, terminal-only and exactly replayable, but every
native transition additionally receives an exogenous stochastic input.  The
experiment asks whether realized stochastic formation can be retained as
generation facts and whether matched counterfactual replay can recover action
credit separately from realized environmental variation.

## Environment and action ancestry

The nine opaque event actions from RL-E03 remain the only actions that enter
terminal-relevant state-component chains.  Six are functional and three are
real formation ancestors with zero scalar credit.  The other 55 actions form
isolated audit outcomes.  They are not falsely bound to terminal-relevant
state results.  This preserves an honest 64-action history and a nine-action
formation-ancestor set.

Every transition also updates eight stochastic state channels.  A stochastic
input is generated by a counter-addressed source keyed by episode seed,
episode identity, realization identity, step and channel name.  It is not read
from a mutable sequential RNG stream.  The same exogenous tape therefore has
the same intervention semantics after an action is changed.

The stochastic channels are transformed at every later native step and are
read only through the final state.  They modulate the non-additive terminal
terms and add a state-formed environmental background term.  Stochasticity is
not terminal noise.  The terminal evaluator reads neither an early action nor
an early random input directly.

## Atomic formation records

For a transition occurrence, prior state, current event action and exogenous
input are represented by separate atomic facts sharing the same concrete
occurrence and the appropriate state-result support.  The stochastic draw is
itself formed in a separate draw occurrence and enters the transition as a
GeneratedOrigin.  Its transition role is
`exogenous_stochastic_input`.  This retains both value identity and the exact
transition occurrence to which it was bound.

Participant-visible facts may not contain causal-credit, oracle-action,
functional/passenger, necessity, backup, substitution, synergy, hidden-target
or expected-credit labels.

## Matched conditional authority

For each episode and exogenous tape, every coalition replay restores the same
initial state, reuses the same occurrence-addressed tape, changes only the
coalition action assignment and executes all 64 native transitions.  Exact
conditional Shapley credit, pair interactions and the pure three-way finite
difference are the conditional authority.

Formation ancestry is only a candidate set.  The three passenger ancestors
must receive zero conditional and expected scalar credit.

## Expected stochastic credit

Conditional exact credits are averaged across independently addressed tapes.
The formal estimate uses a tape count frozen after development.  A disjoint,
larger reference-tape set estimates the held-out stochastic expectation.  The
experiment reports conditional equality separately from expected-credit
sampling error, variance, confidence intervals, sign stability and
interaction stability.  Tapes and candidates within one episode are not
treated as independent episodes for scientific confidence statements.

## Controls

- `exact_matched_reference`: complete scalar `2^k` replay for each estimation tape.
- `gfg_guided_matched`: term-specific formation paths and exact factorization.
- `independent_random_replay`: factual and counterfactual ledgers use distinct tapes; this is a variance comparator, not the causal authority.
- `stochastic_binding_permuted`: the same stochastic source objects and value multiset are rebound to different transition occurrences.
- `trace_history`: development-only flat trace scores with the same candidate budget.
- `temporal_recency`: the last nine actions.
- `formation_ancestry_only`: all ancestors are treated as credited without forks.
- `dependency_dag_supplied_equivalent`: receives the already established term-to-action partition; it does not claim to discover it.
- `rewired_gfg`: term/action paths are rewired and must be rejected or fall back.

Correct stochastic binding is judged on reproduction of the same realized
execution and its conditional credit.  For exchangeable stochastic inputs,
permutation is not required to change the population expectation.

Matched randomness is expected to define a clean conditional contrast.  A
failure to reduce variance relative to independent randomness rejects only the
variance-reduction claim, not the validity of the conditional causal contrast.

## Structure-guided computation

For every fixed tape, exact scalar replay evaluates `2^k` coalitions.  The
GFG-guided method discovers the additive terminal terms and their action
ancestry, evaluates `sum_j 2^(k_j)` exact term games and sums their Shapley
credits by linearity.  Stochastic replication cost, native transitions,
coalitions, cached states, graph construction and end-to-end wall time are all
reported.

## Development and formal separation

Only development seeds may be read while selecting stochastic sample counts,
thresholds and implementation details.  Before formal execution, the contract
must be changed to `FROZEN_BEFORE_FORMAL_EXECUTION`, formal sample counts and
falsification thresholds must be fixed, and SHA-256 identities of executable
sources must be recorded.  No failed formal seed may be removed.

Development used three seeds, four episodes per seed, eight estimation tapes
and 64 disjoint reference tapes.  Before any formal seed was read, the formal
run was frozen to eight seeds, 24 episodes per seed, eight estimation tapes per
episode and 64 disjoint reference tapes per episode.  Exact thresholds and the
SHA-256 identities of `runtime.py`, `runner.py` and `independent_checker.py`
are recorded in `EXPERIMENT_CONTRACT.json`.

The stochastic-credit scientific gates and the computation gates are reported
separately.  Replay transition reduction cannot be used to claim end-to-end
speedup if native capture, canonical GFG validation and the representative
credit-discovery GFG consume the saving.

## Falsification gates

Formal execution can reject the proposed extension if formation-path
retrieval misses action ancestors, conditional factorization differs from the
complete matched authority, passengers receive credit, non-additive
interactions disappear, expected credit is unstable on disjoint tapes,
binding identity is unnecessary for realized replay, downstream learning does
not transfer to unseen stochastic executions, or repeated stochastic
adjudication removes the computational advantage.

## Claim boundary

Success can establish that, in the executed observable and exactly replayable
stochastic long-chain system, realized exogenous inputs can be retained as
generation facts; matched replay can separate action credit from realized
environmental variation; and validated formation structure can preserve exact
conditional credit with lower computation.  It does not establish universal
stochastic temporal-credit assignment or replayability of arbitrary
environments.
