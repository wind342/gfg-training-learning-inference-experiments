# GFG-driven autonomous nanoGPT dual-dynamics discovery

This experiment tests one scoped proposition: whether a general AI, given
only a complete participant-safe generation-fact graph from a real nanoGPT
training run, can submit one sealed executable theory containing distinct
formation and post-formation stability dynamics, forecast an unseen training
run and predict both effects of a preregistered training intervention.

The v7 condition makes stability a primary scientific component rather than a
secondary diagnostic. One candidate contains `FormationDynamics` and
`StabilityDynamics`, plus a unified mechanism that executes both over the full
future grid. Their outputs compose as formation capability minus stability
degradation. The formation transition, full composed curve, degradation-event
precision/recall, deficit curve, recovery timing and causal stability-effect
direction are independently reported; all primary gates must pass for the
same sealed candidate.

It is not a source-optimization benchmark. The earlier nanoGPT experiment is
the frozen execution and capture baseline; this experiment extends it to
long-running, content-addressed training facts and the sequence
discovery -> seal -> unseen forecast -> causal intervention.

The formal-session isolation, five-minute first-contact gate and failure
handling reuse the audited industrial TEP experiment platform at commit
`7057270321839aa417e1b7b7cec6bd38c9732293`. The exact reuse boundary and
nanoGPT-specific adaptations are recorded in
`INDUSTRIAL_PLATFORM_REUSE_AUDIT.md`.

The task-method-oriented v4 condition also requires the same first-contact AI
to read `EXECUTABLE_MECHANISM_DISCOVERY_GUIDE.md` before target evidence is
released. The guide defines state sufficiency, report-code correspondence,
operational state-use auditing, prefix-only falsification and
intervention-state auditing. It also distinguishes a semantically meaningful
state from a state-transition law that has actually survived cross-run
falsification, without disclosing the nanoGPT mechanism or any hidden result.
Earlier completed instances are retained under their original protocols and
are not pooled with the three v4 formal instances.

The v5 semantic-binding rerun preserves every earlier submission and result
but replaces the v4 source-by-outcome fact-block expansion. Every v5 block has
one outcome and only the source-role records that participated in forming that
outcome. The capture separately establishes forward logits and loss, global
gradient norm and per-parameter clipping, each AdamW output component, and
each evaluation output. Full-graph validation rejects multi-outcome blocks and
source/outcome profiles that do not match the frozen capture protocol.

The v6 stability-state-closure condition preserved the v5 generator, semantic
bindings, contracts, hidden evaluation and participant visibility. It adds a
domain-neutral method requirement: post-formation stability must be treated as
an independent state-closure problem, tested at multiple historical cuts for
compressed-state/divergent-future counterexamples, and compiled into the same
executable state law that predicts intervention effects. No target variable,
event time, threshold or hidden answer is supplied by this instruction.

The v7 dual-dynamics condition retains all v6 evidence and results unchanged,
but freezes a new candidate contract. The AI must submit two explicit
executable submodels with separate state and update laws, a full state
trajectory, separate formation and degradation curves, and an exactly composed
validation curve. Stability is evaluated across the full hidden horizon, not
only by a class label or a list of broad intervals. The same frozen causal
intervention must correctly predict both formation shift and stability-effect
direction.

All large tensors, private task material, raw AI sessions and hidden
validation futures are stored under:

`D:/codex/_runs/gfg-nanogpt-capability-stability-discovery-v7-dual-dynamics/`

Contracts, source, tests, commitments, exact sealed candidate submissions and
aggregate public evidence are tracked. The published candidate submissions
contain no hidden future, private tensor archive or raw AI-session transcript.
Formal AI execution is forbidden until the protocol-freeze commit has been
created and pushed.

## Cumulative answer and GFG archive

All historical and future nanoGPT mechanism-discovery answers are organized
under `research_archive/`. Each entry binds the submitted report and
executable candidate to its frozen participant contract, candidate/session
evidence, evaluation revisions and the exact participant-GFG commitment.
Exact GFG bytes are retained in the same local repository under the ignored
`data_private/gfg_nanogpt_mechanism_discovery_archive/` tree; the tracked
archive records their content identities and validation hashes. Formal,
recovery and runtime-repair completion paths automatically append or refresh
the archive index without changing a sealed answer.
